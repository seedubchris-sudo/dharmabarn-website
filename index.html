Why artists book the barn

Video that sounds as good as it looks

Most performance videos are made by camera crews. Ours are made by a recording studio that also films.

Studio-grade audio

Every shoot is multitracked through vintage microphones and discrete preamps, then mixed on the API console.

A room on camera

Timber beams, stage lighting and the 1918 barn give each video its own look, unlike a club or a white studio.

One crew, start to finish

Recording, filming, mixing and editing happen in one place with one team, led by audio/visual director Chris Wright.

Recording & mixing

A world-class signal chain in a barn

Track a band live on the soundstage, overdub in the room, and mix through analog hardware chosen over decades by audio/visual director Chris Wright.

Discuss your project

Services

What we record

Tracking

Full bands live on the soundstage, or instruments and vocals one at a time. Vintage tube microphones into Neve, API and Focusrite preamps.

Mixing

Hybrid analog and digital mixing on the API 1608-II console with Pultec, Manley and Neve processing.

Mastering

A dedicated mastering path through Pultec EQM-1S3 equalizers, the Requisite Audio L2M limiter, Lynx Hilo 2 and SPL Mercury conversion.

Live and livestream audio

Multitrack capture of concerts and livestreams, mixed afterward from the stems so the release sounds finished.
