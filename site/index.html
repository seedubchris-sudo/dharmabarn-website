Dharma Barn

Hygiene, Colorado

Recording, live music films, and intimate events in a historic Colorado barn.

Plan a session

Dharma Barn is a 1918 barn on North 75th Street in Hygiene, between Longmont and Lyons. Today it holds a recording studio, a stage set up for filming, and room for concerts and dinners.

Bands come out to make a record, to film a live session, or both at once.

Recording

We track, mix and master at the barn. A band can set up on the stage and play together, or work through the parts one at a time.

Everything runs through an API 1608-II console. The mics include a vintage Neumann U 47 with its VF14 tube, a vintage AKG C24, a U 67 and a Telefunken ELA M 251 E. Neve, API, Manley and Pultec hardware does most of the rest.

Mastering has its own chain, with Pultec equalizers, a Requisite L2M limiter and Lynx and SPL converters.

Chris Wright runs the studio and the video work.

Video

Shook Twins, live at the barn.

Shows on the stage are filmed on Sony cameras from several angles under stage lights, and each performance is recorded to multitrack at the same time. We mix the audio in the studio first, then cut the picture to it.

Most of what we film is live sessions and concert videos for bands, plus clips for press kits, interviews and podcasts. We can also shoot in Apple Immersive Video for release on Vision Pro.

For a session or a shoot, write to dharmafarm@icloud.com or call Chris at (720) 878-5459.
